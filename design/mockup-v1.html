4Review — flashcards (tap the card)

Spaced repetition under the hood: cards you know come back less often, ones you miss come back sooner. Swipe or tap a grade. Cards don't need a strict Q/A — a single line works too ("Mom's flight lands 3:40 Thursday"); the back is optional.
