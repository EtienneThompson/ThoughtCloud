1Cloud: a live graphINTERACTIVE

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom (or use + / −), and drag a node to pull the web around. Names appear once you zoom in. Tap a node to open it: its neighbours light up and a sheet slides up with actions. Tap a linked chip to jump along the graph.

The big nodes are categories. Items link to their category and to each other: a thought you turned into a todo, two notes you linked, or a todo and the review card for the same trip. Pulsing = due for review, red ring = overdue.

2Review: just read it and swipeINTERACTIVE

No question, no flip. Each card shows the whole thing: a title plus optional detail lines (great for itineraries). Swipe right = "got it", so it waits longer before coming back. Swipe left = "show me again soon". The next card is already underneath.

Since these are often tied to a date, a card can have a "remember until" date. It comes back more often as the date gets close, then archives itself once it has passed.
