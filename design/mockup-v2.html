1Cloud — grouped by categoryNEW

Each cluster is a category. Colour comes from the category, shape shows the type (● thought, ■ todo, ◆ review). Pulsing = due. Group by switches clusters to Type, Due date or Status, and Color can be set separately (e.g. group by Due, color by Category). Tap a cluster to open that category.
